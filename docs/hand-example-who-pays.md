# Hand example: who pays the RO in 2028 (aios-harness#83, Stage 4; RO split amended by point 27, 9 Nov)

Rulings: points 18-20, 22, 27. Facts: 13 (taxpayers' RO share, DESNZ's 41%), 18 (households' share of electricity), 20 (the exemption). All in 2024 prices.

1. **The whole.** The RO line in 2028: **£5,636.8m**; FiT £1,724.2m; Capacity Market (net zero's share) £460.2m, so its whole levy is 460.2 / 14.607% = £3,150.6m.
2. **Households' share.** The RO splits on DESNZ's domestic share of RO costs, "approximately 41 per cent" (point 27, fact 13): households 0.41 x 5,636.8 = **£2,311.1m**, businesses **£3,325.7m**. Every other line keeps households' share of electricity (point 18): 96,188.8 / 275,527.5 GWh = **34.911%** (DUKES 5.1, 2025).
3. **Taxpayers' RO share** (fact 13): the Exchequer funds 75% of households' share through March 2029, so all of 2028: 0.75 x 2,311.1 = **£1,733.3m** moved from households to taxpayers.
4. **Manufacturers' exemption** (fact 20): £600m cash a year from April 2027, all of 2028: 600 / CPIH 1.13113 = **£530.4m**, shared by businesses' part of each scheme: RO 3,325.7, FiT 1,724.2 x 65.089% = 1,122.3, Capacity Market (whole levy) 3,150.6 x 65.089% = 2,050.7, of 6,498.7: RO **£271.4m**, FiT £91.6m, Capacity Market £167.4m of which net zero's share is in the line: £24.4m. Moved from businesses to taxpayers.
5. **RO in 2028, by payer:** households 2,311.1 - 1,733.3 = **£577.8m**; businesses 3,325.7 - 271.4 = **£3,054.3m**; taxpayers 1,733.3 + 271.4 = **£2,004.7m**; together £5,636.8m, the whole.
6. **VAT** (point 18): 5% of what households pay: 0.05 x 577.8 = **£28.9m**, on the VAT line.

## Check

Budget 2025 policy costings, p.12: Exchequer cost £2,580m (2026-27), £2,100m (2027-28), £2,195m (2028-29), cash, on the domestic share at "approximately 41 per cent". With the RO split on the same 41% (point 27), the model's Exchequer cost differs from the Budget's only by the model's RO against the OBR's (the OBR check) and the Budget's own rounding.
