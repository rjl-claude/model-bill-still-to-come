# Hand example: Sizewell C, one charging year from the licence (aios-harness#82, Stage 3)

Licence: Nuclear RAB licence special conditions v7 (`sizewell-c/Nuclear_RAB_Licence_special_conditions_v7.pdf`). Day-1 RAB and the First Charging Year's Allowed Revenue: DESNZ notice of 23 Oct 2025 (`sizewell-c/DESNZ-Day-1-RAB-and-Y1-AR-Letter-to-Ofgem.pdf`). Richard, 5 Nov: count 2040-2099 with stated settings (choice a).

## Charging year 2030-31 (1 April 2030 to 31 March 2031), real CPIH 2024/25

1. **Spend.** Build cost £40.509bn (Original Lower Regulatory Threshold, which the licence measures "when aggregated with the Day-1 RAB", SC28 para 1), less the Day-1 RAB £5,197,977,481.50, spread evenly over the 5,168 days from 4 Nov 2025 to Scheduled COD 29 Dec 2039: **£6,832,628 a day**.
2. **RAB.** Opening (1 Apr 2030, 1,609 days in): 5,197,977,481.50 + 1,609 x 6,832,628 = **£16.192bn**; closing (+365 days) **£18.686bn** (SC27 paras 13, 14).
3. **Return on Capital** (SC31): average RAB x ((1 + 6.73%)^1 - 1) = 17.439 x 0.0673 = **£1,173.6m**.
4. **Opex** (Original Opex Pre-PCR Allowance £2.341bn over the same 5,168 days): 2,341m x 365/5,168 = **£165.3m**.
5. **Social benefits** £10.4m; **FDP allowance** £0.11m (Ofgem 2026 variable values, last year given); **Cost of Debt Adjustment** none after 2027-28 (not yet set).
6. **Tax and the other blocks**, as a share of the Return on Capital, fitted so the model's First Charging Year equals the Secretary of State's £1,443,625,216.26 (nominal; Ofgem's CPIH+forecast index 139.12 for 2025-26, 143.63 for 2026-27, over CPIH 2024/25 134.04): **-7.66%** of the Return on Capital (tax allowances during the build exceed the tax due): -£89.9m.
7. **Allowed Revenue 2030-31:** 1,173.6 + 165.3 + 10.4 + 0.1 - 89.9 = **£1,259.6m** (real 2024/25). Nothing is sold before COD, so the levy is the Allowed Revenue.

## 2040 on (stated settings, choice a)

RAB £40.509bn at COD, written off evenly over 60 years (£675m a year); return 6.73% on the average RAB; running costs BEIS 2016 Nuclear PWR FOAK (2014 prices, x CPIH 2024/2014): fixed £83,400/MW/yr on 3,278 MW, and £12/MWh on 3,278 MW x 90%; power sold at the Scenario's price. Tax after 2039 is not set and is left out (a floor). The levy is the Allowed Revenue less the power sales, both ways.
