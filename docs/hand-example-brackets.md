# Hand example: the last five brackets, and the tornado (aios-harness#85, Stage 6)

Rulings: build plan points 7, 17, 25, 26 and "Output" (3 Nov); Richard, 5 Nov (Sizewell C's return after 2039: 3.5% to 6.73%; fetch Hamilton, Hughes and Generation Costs 2023). Facts 21, 22, 33-35. Each bracket moves one input of the Scenario; the rest stay central.

## 1. Forward prices, then Annex M (point 26, fact 21)

Season-ahead baseload prices (LCCC BMRP, cash): Winter 2025 (Oct 2025-Mar 2026) £84.75; Summer 2026 (Apr-Sep) £75.30; Winter 2026 (Oct 2026-Mar 2027) £114.50/MWh.

- **2026**, by months: (3 x 84.75 + 6 x 75.30 + 3 x 114.50) / 12 = £87.4625 cash; / CPIH 1.07197 = **£81.59/MWh** (2024 prices), against Annex M's £75.26.
- **2027**: Jan-Mar at 114.50 / CPIH 1.10739 = £103.40; Apr-Dec at Annex M's £62.66: (3 x 103.40 + 9 x 62.66) / 12 = **£72.84/MWh**, against £62.66.
- 2028 on: Annex M, unchanged.

Higher prices mean smaller CfD top-ups, so this end lowers the bill: low = forward, high = central.

| End | Wind | Solar PV |

## 3. Output level, high end (build plan "Output"; fact 35)

Every CfD wind and solar farm takes DESNZ's 2023 load factor for the commissioning year nearest its start (2025 for any earlier start): an offshore farm starting 2028 takes 2030's **65%**; one starting 2033 takes 2035's 69%; onshore 2024 takes 45%; solar 11%. Remote island wind as onshore. RO and FiT stay on their own record (Richard, 5 Nov). Decline with age applies as at central.

## 4. Sizewell C's return after 2039 (Richard, 5 Nov)

Low **3.5%** (the Green Book rate before its 28 Oct 2026 revision; kept as Richard set it, 5 Nov), high central 6.73%. In 2040 (n = 0) at £40.509bn: depreciation 40.509 / 60 = £0.675bn; average RAB 40.509 - 0.675 / 2 = £40.171bn; return 6.73% x 40.171 = £2.704bn at central, 3.5% x 40.171 = **£1.406bn** at the low end (2024/25 prices, before running costs and power sold).

## 5. Build path (points 7, 25)

The four NESO FES 2025 pathways in turn as the wind and solar build path; the ends are the pathways giving the lowest and highest bill, found by running.

## The tornado

Each bracket's two ends are run, the rest central. For each: the whole bill at each end, its swing (high minus low), ranked by swing. The range is the lowest and highest bill of any bracket run. A bracket whose two ends give the same bill moves nothing, and fails.
