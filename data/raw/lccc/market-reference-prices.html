Contracts for Difference

Access Market Reference Price information used to calculate Contracts for Difference payments, including Baseload Market Reference Prices and Intermittent Market Reference Prices.

  2. Our Schemes

  4. Market Reference Prices

Market Reference Prices

Market Reference Prices are used to calculate CfD Generator payments. There are two classifications of generation under a CfD contract and each has its own Market Reference Price:

  • Baseload Technologies (such as biomass with CHP)
  • Intermittent Technologies (such as solar or wind)

Baseload Market Reference Price

The BMRP is calculated by LCCC on a seasonal basis, pursuant to condition 15 of the Contract for Difference Standard Terms and Conditions. The BMRP is a volume-weighted average based on forward-season trading data received from LEBA. The seasonal BMRP is published in April and October of each year.

Intermittent Market Reference Price (IMRP)

The Intermittent Market Reference Price is calculated using day-ahead data received from EPEX SPOT and N2EX. An IMRP is calculated for every hour of the day pursuant to condition 21 of the Contract for Difference Standard Terms and Conditions.
