## 1.2.6 (14 Nov 2026), Northern Ireland's VAT window

The independent checker of 1.2.5 (14 Nov 2026, `report-series/price-of-the-climate-bill/handover/checker-model-1.2.5.md`, section D) found one fault: the Northern Ireland upper bound assumed Northern Ireland pays 5% until 30 June 2027. SI 2026/987 keeps Northern Ireland at the reduced rate for supplies from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027 (art. 1(2), art. 4). Budget 2026 says "Extended VAT relief is expected to apply in Northern Ireland from April 2027" (`hmt/budget-2026.pdf` para 3.4, printed p.43, pdf p.49) and "This is expected to apply UK-wide from April 2027" (para 6.210, printed p.132, pdf p.138). The model counts the Budget's extension for Great Britain, so on the same basis Northern Ireland is at 0% from April 2027, and the window the model overstates is 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027 only (92 days of 2026 and 90 of 2027).

- `vat.zero_rate.northern_ireland_upper_bound.cash`: £5.92m to £4.00m (5% x the days in the order's window, `vat_rate(y, VAT_ZERO_IN_LAW)`, in place of the modelled window). Its `.words` now say what the order does and to when, what Budget 2026 says with its paragraphs, and the full reason the figure is an upper bound: in the year to March 2025 Northern Ireland's stations were issued 5.6 million certificates (the RO certificates register, compliance period 2024/2025) and Northern Ireland's suppliers had to present 1.4 million (Ofgem RO annual report SY23, Figure 5.1 and para 5.3: 0.192 ROCs/MWh on 7.26 TWh); 6.1 million Northern Ireland certificates were redeemed against that year (the compliance certificates register), so at least 4.7 million were presented by suppliers in Great Britain. Its unit (`key_units`) says the window.
- Nothing else moves: every other key equals 1.2.5 exactly (7,195 keys); no key is added or removed. Every key: `docs/figures-1.2.5-vs-1.2.6.csv`; in plain words: `docs/figures-1.2.5-vs-1.2.6.md`; tests: `tests/test_version_1_2_6.py` against the frozen 1.2.5 file (`tests/fixtures/figures-1.2.5.json`, sha256 c7ed3a41…).
- Tests (the checker's section E): the Northern Ireland test is now a hand calculation from the run's own rows (`results/results.csv`, `results/ro_stations.csv`), not the code's formula restated; `tests/test_version_1_2_5.py` reads the frozen 1.2.5 file, its Northern Ireland test is the same hand calculation for 1.2.5's window, and its meta test allows only what its name says (the version, the MANIFEST fingerprint and new units, with no old unit changed). `tests/test_freeze.py`: version 1.2.6.
- The 1.2.5 entry below, `docs/figures-1.2.4-vs-1.2.5.md` and the 1.2.5 handover stay as written ("the order leaves Northern Ireland at 5%" without the Budget's Northern Ireland sentence); this entry and `docs/design.md` (1.2.6 section) correct them.

## 1.2.5 (13 Nov 2026), VAT's zero rate

In law VAT on domestic electricity in Great Britain is 0% for supplies from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027 (the Value Added Tax (Supplies of Domestic Electricity) Order 2026, SI 2026/987, made 7 Sep 2026, art. 1(2), saved in `data/raw/legislation/`); Budget 2026 (28 Oct 2026; `hmt/budget-2026.pdf` para 2.48 and Box 3.A; `hmt/AB26_Policy_Costing.pdf` p.12, "effective from 1 October 2026 until 1 July 2027") announced its extension, not yet law at the cut-off. On 13 Nov 2026 legislation.gov.uk listed no extending order. **1.2.5 counts 0% from 1 October 2026 to 30 June 2027**, as the model counts the Budget's other announced measures (conductor's ruling, 11 Nov 2026, aios-harness#236), by days in each calendar year (`who_pays.vat_rate`: 2026 at 5% on 273 of 365 days, 2027 on 184), in cash and in both present values.
